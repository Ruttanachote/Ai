## Who they are and what video types they make

### Takeaway
Fox Funnels is a Bangkok "performance creative & paid ads" agency, not a funnel-software company. It sells three video formats: commercial, UGC (creator-style) and hybrid. It also has an "AI-Powered Video" portfolio category, and it says AI is used to turn each shoot into many ad variations.

### Cited Findings
- The site title is "Performance Creative & Paid Ads Agency Bangkok". It serves Thai and international clients and claims "Trusted by 200+ brands since 2019" and a 4.9 Google Reviews rating. — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/)
- The homepage lists three formats: commercial, UGC ("authentic creator-style content") and hybrid (a mix). It says it "combines AI with real production to deliver performance video — commercial, UGC, hybrid — faster and at scale." — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/)
- Portfolio categories are ads, UGC, brand films and AI video, including an "AI-Powered Video" category. The portfolio claims "300+ performance videos" across UGC, commercials and AI-powered creative. — [Portfolio](https://fox-funnels.com/portfolio)
- UGC page: the agency "sources and briefs creators, scripts hooks from real customer language, and uses AI-assisted production" to turn "every shoot into dozens of platform-ready ad variations for TikTok and Meta". It claims about 10x the deliverables of a traditional UGC package at the same shoot cost. — [UGC Ads Agency](https://fox-funnels.com/ugc-ads-agency)
- TikTok page: it produces TikTok UGC ads in-house, so clients don't recruit or manage creators themselves. — [TikTok Ads Agency](https://fox-funnels.com/tiktok-ads-agency)
- Performance video page (quoted in a snippet): "one concept becomes dozens of testable variations." — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/)
- Paid media: it plans, launches and scales campaigns on Meta, TikTok and Google. — [Paid Ads Management Bangkok](https://fox-funnels.com/paid-ads-management-bangkok); [Facebook Ads Agency Bangkok](https://fox-funnels.com/facebook-ads-agency-bangkok)
- The Facebook page is named "Fox-Funnels - Video Marketing Agency" and has a Reels tab. It lists Bang Na, Bangkok, while ZoomInfo lists Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya. — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/foxfunnels/); [FB Reels](https://www.facebook.com/foxfunnels/reels/); [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/pic/fox-funnels/546747397)

### Inferences
- These are mostly client ad creatives, not the agency's own funnel VSLs. Expect real creators filmed on location in Thailand, then edited by AI into many variants (different hooks, cuts and captions). Fully AI-avatar ads are probably a smaller "AI video" line.
- The "hybrid" format probably mixes polished commercial footage with creator-style talking-head or selfie segments.

## Script structure and hooks

### Takeaway
The site says hooks are written "from real customer language" and that hook and angle testing runs for 2–4 weeks. We found no verbatim hook or script line from an actual video.

### Cited Findings
- "Scripts hooks from real customer language." — [UGC Ads Agency](https://fox-funnels.com/ugc-ads-agency)
- The site says it takes two to four weeks of testing to learn "which hooks and angles your market responds to". — [fox-funnels.com search snippet](https://fox-funnels.com/ugc-ads-agency)
- Case study claim: MakeMyPatch went "from losing money on every sale to 4.1x ROAS in 4 months, with creator-style ad testing doing the heavy lifting" (0.60x to 4.1x). — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/); [Portfolio](https://fox-funnels.com/portfolio)
- Case study claim: Jet's Italian Deli got an 8.4x blended ROAS from performance video plus managed paid social. — [Facebook Ads Agency page](https://fox-funnels.com/facebookads)
- The site claims "$5M+ ad spend managed" and "300+ videos produced". — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/)

### Inferences
- Their method looks like standard direct-response UGC: mine reviews and comments for real customer wording, write several hooks for one body and CTA, and test them on Meta and TikTok.
- A problem, agitate, solution, CTA structure is likely, but we have not seen it in their videos.

### Gaps
- No verbatim hooks, no first-3-second lines, no CTA wording and no transcripts.
