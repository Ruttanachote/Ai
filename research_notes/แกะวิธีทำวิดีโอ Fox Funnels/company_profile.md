# Fox Funnels (fox-funnels.com): Company Profile

Research date: 2026-11-01.

**Method and its limits.** This research session could not reach fox-funnels.com directly. curl got a 403 when opening the connection through the egress proxy, and WebFetch could not resolve the hostname (ENOTFOUND). web.archive.org and archive.org were also blocked. So **no raw HTML, robots.txt, sitemap.xml or Wayback snapshot was inspected**. Everything below comes from search-engine indexing of fox-funnels.com pages (titles and snippets), LinkedIn, ZoomInfo, RocketReach and Facebook result snippets. A source tagged "site" means the claim appeared in indexed text from fox-funnels.com, not that I loaded and read the page. To get the tech-stack and video-host details, fox-funnels.com and web.archive.org must be allowed in the session's network settings.

**Entity identified (high confidence).** Fox-Funnels / Fox Funnels is a small digital-marketing and performance-creative agency in Bangkok, Thailand, at fox-funnels.com. It is not FunnelFox (funnelfox.com), a separate web2app funnel SaaS that shows up in name searches ([FunnelFox](https://funnelfox.com/docs/getting-started/quickstart)). It is also not "Foxys Funnels", a food vendor on Facebook ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/foxysfunnels/)).

## What exactly is Fox Funnels, and which market and language?

### Takeaway
Fox Funnels is a done-for-you agency in Bangkok. It produces performance video creative (commercial, UGC and hybrid, with AI-assisted variation) and then runs the paid ads itself on Meta, TikTok, Google and LINE. It serves Thai and international clients and works in English and Thai. It is not SaaS and not a course.

## Which video offers do they sell, and at what prices?

### Takeaway
They sell done-for-you **performance ad video** in three formats (commercial, UGC and hybrid), with AI-assisted multiplication into many test variants. It is bundled with ad management on Meta, TikTok, Google and LINE. **No public pricing was found.** The sales path is a free growth audit or a contact form.

### Cited Findings
- Three production types are offered:
  - **Commercial**: high-end brand and product films
  - **UGC**: authentic creator-style content
  - **Hybrid**: a mix of the two

  — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/)
- On the UGC process, they say they source and brief creators, script hooks from real customer language, and use AI-assisted production to turn every shoot into dozens of platform-ready ad variations for TikTok and Meta. — [UGC Ads Agency](https://fox-funnels.com/ugc-ads-agency)
- AI is used to turn one concept into many test variations: different hooks, openings, lengths, aspect ratios and languages. The site claims about 10x the deliverables of a traditional UGC package at the same shoot cost. — [UGC Ads Agency](https://fox-funnels.com/ugc-ads-agency) (self-reported)
- It claims to be "the only performance agency in Bangkok with an AI-powered production engine", with production costs about 10x lower than US or UK agencies. — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/) (unverified marketing claim)
- The UGC page offers a free growth audit as its call to action. — [UGC Ads Agency](https://fox-funnels.com/ugc-ads-agency)
- The Meta page describes full-funnel campaigns (cold prospecting, retargeting and retention) with pixel and Conversions API tracking. — [Facebook Ads Agency Bangkok](https://fox-funnels.com/facebook-ads-agency-bangkok)
- Advice from the older /facebookads article: a strong offer is "extremely important", and a low Add-to-cart to ViewContent ratio means the offer is weak, so test prices and offers. — [facebookads](https://www.fox-funnels.com/facebookads/)
- The paid ads page says accounts fail because of weak offers and worn-out creative. — [Paid Ads Management](https://fox-funnels.com/paid-ads-management-bangkok)

### Inferences
- Their core method: shoot a small amount of real footage (creator or commercial), then use AI editing and variation to produce many hook, length, ratio and language variants for creative testing on Meta and TikTok. Whether they use **AI avatars** or synthetic presenters is not stated in the indexed text. "AI-assisted production" may mean editing, variation, voice or translation, not fully synthetic video.
- No standalone VSL product was found in current pages. "Automated sales funnels" (LinkedIn) suggests they built funnels with VSL-style video in the past.

### Gaps
- Prices, packages and retainers: not public in any indexed page.
- The specific AI tools used (for example voice cloning, avatars or translation): not disclosed in snippets.

## Are there testimonials, case studies, reviews or ad-library entries?

### Takeaway
The site has self-published case studies: MakeMyPatch (ROAS 0.60x to 4.1x) and an unnamed Bangkok F&B brand (8.4x blended ROAS), plus a testimonial from Alberto of Baci Baci. I found no independent reviews (Trustpilot, Google or Reddit), and only one Facebook review was mentioned.

### Cited Findings
- **MakeMyPatch** (e-commerce): ROAS went from 0.60x to 4.1x and acquisition cost fell 69% after "four months of relentless creative testing and rebuilt campaigns". — [Facebook Ads Agency Bangkok](https://fox-funnels.com/facebook-ads-agency-bangkok); [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/)
- **Unnamed local Bangkok F&B brand**: 8.4x blended ROAS. — [facebookads](https://www.fox-funnels.com/facebookads/)
- **Testimonial** from Alberto (Founder, Baci Baci): credits the agency with taking the brand from zero to 8.4x blended ROAS and more than 850 orders a month. — [fox-funnels.com](https://fox-funnels.com/). The site does not say explicitly whether Baci Baci is the unnamed F&B brand.
- **Volume claims**: 300+ videos produced, 200+ brands, $5M+ in ad spend managed. — [Portfolio](https://fox-funnels.com/portfolio); [TikTok Ads Agency](https://fox-funnels.com/tiktok-ads-agency)
- No Trustpilot, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or review-site results were found for Fox-Funnels Bangkok. An earlier search summary mentions only a single Facebook review. — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/foxfunnels/)

### Inferences
- The F&B 8.4x figure and the Baci Baci testimonial very likely describe the same client, given the matching 8.4x figure and the F&B context. This is unconfirmed.
- All performance figures are self-reported and unaudited.

### Gaps
